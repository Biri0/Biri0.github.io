Privacy Policy for Dialettario

Effective Date: August 22, 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how Dialettario ("we," "our," or "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile application (the "App"). By using Dialettario, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Personal Information

When you sign in with Google, we collect:

  • Your Google account name
  • Your Google account email address
  • Your Google profile picture (if available)
  • A unique user identifier from Google

1.2 Content Data

When you use our App, we collect:

  • Dialect definitions you submit
  • Example usage sentences you provide
  • Location information (Italian regions, provinces, and cities) you select
  • Timestamps of when content is created or modified

1.3 Technical Data

We may automatically collect basic technical information necessary for the App to function:

  • Device type and operating system version (for compatibility)
  • App crash reports (for bug fixes and stability)

1.4 Advertising Data

Our App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and use advertising identifiers to show you personalized ads. This includes:

  • Advertising ID (IDFA on iOS, AAID on Android)
  • Device and network information for ad targeting
  • Interaction data with advertisements

2. How We Use Your Information

2.1 To Provide Our Service

  • Authenticate your account and maintain user sessions
  • Store and display your dialect contributions
  • Enable location-based filtering of content
  • Ensure the App functions properly on your device

2.2 To Display Content

  • Show dialect definitions relevant to your selected regions
  • Maintain attribution for user-generated content

2.3 For Advertising Purposes

  • Display advertisements through Google AdMob
  • Enable personalized advertising based on your interests
  • Support the free operation of our App

3. Information Sharing and Disclosure

3.1 Public Content

Important: Any dialect definitions, examples, and associated location information you submit will be publicly visible to all App users. Your name (from your Google account) will be displayed as the contributor of the content.

3.2 Third-Party Services

We share information with the following third-party services:

  • Google Authentication: For user authentication and account management
  • Google AdMob: For advertising services and personalized ads
  • Database Hosting: For secure storage of user data and content

3.3 Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required to do so by law or in response to valid requests by public authorities.

4. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

5. Data Retention

  • Account Data: Retained while your account is active
  • Content Data: Dialect definitions and examples are retained indefinitely to maintain the public dictionary

6. Your Rights and Choices

6.1 Account Control

  • You can sign out of your account at any time
  • You can request deletion of your account through the Profile page in the app

6.2 Data Deletion

Account Deletion: You can delete your account and all associated data directly through the app by:

  • Mobile App: Go to Profile → Zona di pericolo → "Elimina account"
  • Web App: Go to Profile → Zona di pericolo → "Elimina account"
  • Email Request: Contact us at marianorief@gmail.com

When you delete your account, we will permanently remove:

  • Your personal information (name, email, profile picture)
  • Your authentication data and session information
  • All definitions, examples, votes, and comments you've submitted
  • Any other data associated with your account

Note: Account deletion is immediate and cannot be undone. All your contributions to the dialect dictionary will be permanently removed.

6.3 Advertising Choices

  • You can opt out of personalized ads through your device settings
  • iOS: Settings > Privacy & Security > Apple Advertising > Personalized Ads
  • Android: Settings > Google > Ads > Opt out of Ads Personalization

6.4 Content Management

Once submitted, dialect definitions become part of the public dictionary. If you need to modify or remove content, please contact us directly.

7. Age Requirements

Our App is suitable for users of all ages. However, users under the age of 18 should have parental consent before creating an account or submitting content to our platform.

8. International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your country of residence. These countries may have different data protection laws than your country.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Effective Date" at the top. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.

10. Third-Party Privacy Policies

This Privacy Policy does not apply to other companies' advertisements or websites. We encourage you to read the privacy policies of these third parties:

  • Google Privacy Policy (for Google Sign-In and AdMob)
  • AdMob Privacy Information
