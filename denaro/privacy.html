Privacy Policy - Denaro App

Last updated: December 2024

In summary: Denaro is a completely offline app. We do not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. All your financial data remains exclusively on your device.

1. Who we are

Denaro is a mobile application for personal finance management designed to work completely offline. The app is developed with the goal of ensuring maximum privacy and security of user data.

2. Data we do NOT collect

Denaro is designed to be completely offline. We do not collect, transmit, share, or sell:

  • Financial information (balances, transactions, buckets)
  • Personally identifiable information
  • App usage information
  • Location data
  • Device information
  • Biometric data or security PINs
  • Language preferences or other settings

3. Where your data is stored

All data you enter in Denaro is stored exclusively:

  • On your local device in an encrypted SQLite database
  • In the device's secure storage for security settings (PIN)
  • In the app's local storage for preferences (language, theme)

We do not use cloud servers, automatic backup services, or remote databases.

4. Data security

Your data is protected through:

  • Database encryption: the SQLite database is encrypted with randomly generated keys
  • Local authentication: PIN and biometric authentication managed exclusively by the operating system
  • App isolation: data is accessible only by the Denaro app on your device
  • No network transmission: the app never sends data over the internet

5. Optional security features

The app offers optional security features:

  • Access PIN: stored securely on the device using Expo SecureStore
  • Biometric authentication: managed directly by the device's operating system

This security data is never transmitted or shared.

6. App permissions

Denaro only requests the minimum necessary permissions:

  • Local storage: to save financial data on the device
  • Biometric authentication (optional): if you choose to enable this feature

We do not request access to contacts, camera, microphone, location, or other sensitive data.

7. Backup and synchronization

Denaro does not offer cloud backup or automatic synchronization features. You are responsible for backing up your data through:

  • Device backup (if supported by the operating system)
  • Future export tools we may add

8. Third-party services

Denaro does not integrate analytics, advertising, or other third-party services that could collect data. The app is completely self-contained.

9. User rights

Since we do not collect personal data, you always have complete control:

  • Access: all your data is visible in the app
  • Modification: you can edit or delete any data directly in the app
  • Deletion: uninstalling the app removes all associated data
  • Portability: your data remains on your device

10. Changes to this Privacy Policy

Any changes to this privacy policy will be communicated through:

  • App updates in the stores
  • In-app notifications (if implemented)
  • Publication of the updated version

11. Regulatory compliance

Denaro is designed to comply with:

  • GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation)
  • CCPA (California Consumer Privacy Act)
  • Other privacy regulations

Compliance is facilitated by the fact that we do not collect personal data.
