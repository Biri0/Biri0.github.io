Privacy Policy for PplRate

Last updated: August 2025
Privacy First: PplRate does not collect, store, or transmit any of your personal data. Everything stays on your device, encrypted and private.

1. Introduction

Welcome to PplRate. This Privacy Policy explains our commitment to your privacy and how we handle information in our mobile application. PplRate is designed with privacy as the core principle - we simply do not collect your personal data.

2. What We DON'T Collect

PplRate does not collect, store, transmit, or have access to:

  • Your personal notes or ratings about people
  • Your contact information or imported contacts
  • Your usage patterns or app behavior
  • Your device information or identifiers
  • Your location or personal data of any kind
  • Analytics, crash reports, or telemetry data

3. How Your Data is Stored

3.1 Local Storage Only

All your data is stored exclusively on your device using:

  • SQLCipher encryption: Your database is encrypted with industry-standard 256-bit AES encryption
  • Device-only storage: Data never leaves your device
  • Secure key management: Encryption keys are stored in your device's secure keychain

3.2 No Cloud, No Servers

PplRate operates completely offline and does not:

  • Connect to any servers
  • Upload or sync data to the cloud
  • Transmit information over the internet
  • Create backups on external systems

4. Contact Access

When you choose to import contacts:

  • This happens only when you explicitly request it
  • Contact data is read locally from your device
  • No contact information is transmitted anywhere
  • You can revoke this permission at any time in device settings
  • The app works fully without contact access

5. Third-Party Services

5.1 Google AdMob

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect advertising-related data according to Google's privacy policy. This data collection is handled entirely by Google, not by us.

Learn more about Google's advertising practices: https://policies.google.com/privacy

You can opt out of personalized advertising in your device settings.

5.2 No Other Third Parties

PplRate does not integrate with any other third-party services, analytics platforms, or data collection tools.

6. Data Security

Your data security is ensured through:

  • Local encryption: All data is encrypted on your device
  • No network transmission: Data never travels over the internet
  • App isolation: Other apps cannot access PplRate's data
  • Secure deletion: Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all data

7. Your Rights

Since we don't collect your data, traditional privacy rights work differently:

  • Data Access: All your data is always accessible within the app
  • Data Deletion: Delete individual items or uninstall the app to remove everything
  • Data Portability: Your data remains on your device for you to manage
  • No Data Requests: Since we have no access to your data, we cannot provide copies or delete data from our systems

8. Age Requirements

PplRate is suitable for users of all ages. There are no age restrictions.

9. Changes to This Policy

Any changes to this privacy policy will be communicated through app updates. The fundamental principle of local-only, no-collection privacy will not change.

11. Legal Compliance

This privacy-first approach ensures compliance with:

  • GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation)
  • CCPA (California Consumer Privacy Act)
  • Other international privacy regulations

Since no personal data is collected, most privacy regulations do not apply to PplRate's core functionality.

12. Open Source Transparency

PplRate's privacy claims can be verified through code review. The app's architecture is designed to make data collection technically impossible.

Summary: PplRate is designed to be completely private. Your notes, ratings, and personal information never leave your device. We cannot access, collect, or see any of your data - by design, not just by policy.
